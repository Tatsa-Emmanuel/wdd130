Wild Waters Rafting

At Wild Waters Rafting, our mission is to connect people with the raw beauty and excitement of nature through safe, unforgettable white water experiences. We believe every rapid tells a story — and we are here to help you live yours. Our certified guides are passionate about adventure and committed to your safety every step of the way.

History

Wild Waters Rafting was founded in 1998 by two lifelong river enthusiasts, Maria and Tom, who dreamed of sharing their love of the outdoors with the world. Starting with just two rafts and a battered pickup truck, they led their first trip down the Snake River with a group of eight brave souls. Word spread quickly. By 2005, Wild Waters had grown to a full fleet of rafts and a team of 20 expert guides. Today, we operate across five premier river destinations and have safely guided over 50,000 adventurers of all skill levels. Our commitment to excellence, environmental stewardship, and genuine fun has remained unchanged since day one.
