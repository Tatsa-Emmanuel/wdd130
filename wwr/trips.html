Half-Day Sprint

Perfect for beginners and families with children. This 3-hour journey takes you through Class II and mild Class III rapids. Enjoy a refreshing swim break and gorgeous canyon views.

Full Throttle Day

Ready for an adrenaline rush? This 6-hour intense excursion navigates continuous Class III and IV rapids, including the famous 'Dientes del Diablo'. Lunch is provided on the riverbank.

Multi-Day Expedition

Disconnect from the world with our 3-day, 2-night guided expedition. Experience the river by day and sleep under the stars by night. All camping gear, food, and safety equipment are included.
